Mission Briefing

Eight category slots sit open on your grid: Population, Land Mass, Military Budget, Coastline, GDP, Airports, Internet Access, and Renewable Energy. Nations get pulled from the global pool one at a time — you decide which open slot each one fills before it locks for good. Once it's full, name your country and spin the reel for a real-world battlefield.

You'll still draft your 8-stat grid the same way. The difference comes after: instead of naming your country and spinning for a random region, you'll spin a reel that lands on a real country — you take on its name and its real-world neighbors become your rivals.
