Mission Briefing

Eight category slots sit open on your grid: Population, Land Mass, Military Budget, Coastline, GDP, Airports, Internet Speed, and Renewable Energy. Nations get pulled from the global pool one at a time — you decide which open slot each one fills before it locks for good. Once it's full, name your country and spin the reel for a real-world battlefield.

You'll still draft your 8-stat grid the same way. The difference comes after: instead of naming your country and spinning for a random region, you'll spin a reel that lands on a real country — you take on its name and its real-world neighbors become your rivals.

One shot a day. Everyone playing today draws from the same shuffled deck and becomes the same country, so it's rivals-for-rivals the same fight for everybody — no region luck, no easier draw. Locked to Hard difficulty with stats hidden so it's a fair, skill-only leaderboard.

Draft one grid, then defend it region after region, back-to-back, with no do-overs — the same country stats stay locked in the whole way through. Win and the reel spins again for a new, unfaced region; lose once and the run ends. Your streak (how many regions you survived) is what gets submitted to the Gauntlet leaderboard.

📜 Changelog

v1.1
  • 🔥 Gauntlet mode — draft once, defend that grid region after region until your first loss; validated and tracked on its own Gauntlet leaderboard
  • ⚔️ Multiplayer — 1v1 Duel mode: create a room, send the code to a friend, both draft independently, whoever built the stronger grid wins (server-recomputed, not client-trusted)
  • Duel host now configures difficulty and stats-visibility for the room — both players draft under the same rules
  • Duel results now play out through the same narrated battle-log animation as solo campaigns, instead of jumping straight to the result
  • Rematch button after a duel — both players confirm and the same room resets straight back into a fresh draft, no new code needed
  • Removed Weekly Theme (the regional restriction never earned its keep) — all reels now always draw from the full region/country pool
  • Fixed a bug where the Gauntlet's spin button stayed disabled after the first round, making it impossible to continue a streak
  • Fixed a bug where the previous round's "Deploy" button could stay active between Gauntlet rounds
v1.0 — Full Release
  • Full dataset rebuilt from real published sources: World Bank (population), IMF (GDP), Ookla Speedtest Global Index (internet speed), SIPRI/IISS (military spending), Ember/IEA (renewable energy) — no more placeholder numbers
  • Dynamic per-region Home Field Advantage rebalanced against the real dataset, so which region the reel lands on matters far less than how well you drafted
  • Reworked score formula — rewards decisive performance and margin, not just a win/loss coin flip
  • 🔥 Gauntlet mode — draft once, defend that grid region after region until your first loss; streaks tracked on their own leaderboard
  • 🎯 Weekly Theme — a rotating regional theme every week (with an opt-out toggle to ignore it and play anywhere)
  • 🗓️ Daily Challenge as a full standalone mode — same country, same rivals for everyone that day, one attempt only, locked to Hard difficulty with hidden stats for a fair fight
  • Separate username + country name, both shown on the leaderboard
  • Profanity filter on names — live as you type, and re-checked server-side so it can't be bypassed
  • Server-side score validation — a Supabase Edge Function independently recomputes every submitted score (and every Gauntlet round) from the raw draft choices before anything is written, so scores can't be faked from the browser console
  • All-Time, This Week, Daily, and Gauntlet leaderboard tabs, each showing your own runs highlighted
  • "This Week" leaderboard resets Monday midnight New Zealand time
  • Back to Menu button that preserves your mode/difficulty settings; Run New Campaign resumes them automatically unless you just played Daily Challenge
  • Rolling tumble animation when a new nation is dealt into the draft or rerolled
  • Land Mass and Population now display in sensible units (k km² / M km², M / B) instead of always in thousands or millions
  • Simplified difficulty from three tiers to two — Normal and Hard (Easy mode removed)
v0.5 (BETA)
  • 8-category draft grid: Population, Land Mass, Military Budget, Coastline, GDP, Airports, Internet Speed, Renewable Energy
  • Two game modes: Build My Own Country, or Become a Country (adopt a real nation's name and neighbors after drafting)
  • Easy / Medium / Hard difficulty with rerolls and blind-draft stat hiding
  • Region Reel spinner across 19 real-world sub-regions (or a Country Reel in Become mode)
  • Name Your Country before deploying
  • Campaign simulation with Home Field Advantage and a narrated, explained battle log
  • Category-by-category clash bars and an overall power meter
  • Copy-and-paste shareable score report
  • Light and dark mode (dark by default)
  • Sound effects, victory confetti, and defeat shake
  • Personal high score tracker (saved on this device)
  • Global leaderboard by difficulty, powered by Supabase
  • Interactive tutorial (opens from the ❓ button — no longer auto-pops)
  • Restart Run button with confirmation
  • Home screen app icon for mobile install
