Mission Briefing

Eight category slots sit open on your grid: Population, Land Mass, Military Budget, Coastline, GDP, Airports, Internet Access, and Renewable Energy. Nations get pulled from the global pool one at a time — you decide which open slot each one fills before it locks for good.

Once your grid is full, spin the region reel for a real-world battlefield. Three native rival nations rise to defend it, each boosted by Home Field Advantage. Choose your difficulty below, then begin.

Easy
3 rerolls · all stats visible while drafting

Hard
No rerolls · stats hidden — draft blind

Draft Grid
